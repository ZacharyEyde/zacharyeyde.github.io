---
title: Automated DES & Digital Twin Generation
description: How simulation models and digital twins can be discovered from data
importance: 4
category: research
---

Simulation models and digital twins for flexible manufacturing are usually built by hand, which makes them slow to create and hard to keep in sync with the real system. This project surveys methods that discover or generate these models automatically. It is organized around three questions:

1. **Formalisms:** which model representations are being discovered (Petri nets, ERGs, DEVS, stochastic models, and others)
2. **Data and methods:** which data sources and techniques drive discovery, from process mining and object-centric process mining to LLM-augmented approaches
3. **Performance:** the computational and communication costs of running these models as live digital twins
