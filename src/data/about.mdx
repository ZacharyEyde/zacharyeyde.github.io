I'm an engineering PhD student at Arizona State University and Senior Technical Staff in Intel's Sort/Test Technology Development group. My research is on discrete-event simulation (DES) and digital twins for semiconductor test and manufacturing, especially generating simulation models automatically from operational data instead of building them by hand.

At Intel I work on test quality, automated inspection, and AI/ML for test, including physics-informed neural networks for thermal modeling. I'm also the Intel mentor and Co-PI for the Intel–ASU research collaboration on generative digital twins for semiconductor test operations, with Giulia Pedrielli at ASU's School of Computing and Augmented Intelligence.

My background spans mechanical, electrical, and industrial engineering, with master's degrees in all three, and I'm a licensed Professional Engineer in Arizona. Before Intel, I designed radomes, antennas, and RF packaging at Raytheon Missile Systems.

**Research interests**

- Automated discovery and generation of DES models and digital twins
- Process mining and event relationship graphs (ERGs)
- Physics-informed machine learning for semiconductor test and thermal modeling
- Simulation-based optimization for semiconductor assembly and test
